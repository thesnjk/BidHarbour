Alongside the MISSING list for {{buyer_or_pack_label}} (pack {{pack_id}}), a short note on how Supplier Desk treats gaps:

1. We match your evidence library to each requirement. Where we have no approved evidence, the row stays **MISSING** — we will not invent certifications, insurance, financials, or capabilities.
2. That is deliberate. Public tender / AI-draft tools often fill prose to look complete. Our job is inbound customer packs with evidence behind every statement, or an honest gap.
3. When you send evidence (or confirm not held / N/A with a short rationale) via the agreed encrypted path, we rematch and continue toward Approver review.
4. Export still needs your named Approver ({{Approver}}) to Approve — we do not portal auto-submit.

{{Founder}}
