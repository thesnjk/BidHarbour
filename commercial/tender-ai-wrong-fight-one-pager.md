## Goal

One scan-ready comparison: tender-AI tools help SMEs **find and draft** public (and some private) tenders to win contracts; Supplier Desk helps suppliers assemble each **inbound customer qualification pack** with evidence match and honest gaps. Different job — do not compete on win-rate drafting. Copycat watch: tender vendor adding “customer pack importer” ([`../research/copycat-monitor-quarterly.md`](../research/copycat-monitor-quarterly.md)).
