## Goal

One scan-ready comparison: Evidash helps **buyers** cover and validate vendor documents; Supplier Desk helps **suppliers** assemble each inbound customer questionnaire with checklist → evidence match → MISSING/EXPIRED/CONFLICT → named Approver → export. Wrong side of the market for Norfolk SME suppliers — complementary jobs if a large buyer already uses Evidash, not a substitute.
