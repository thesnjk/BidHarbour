# Outreach sequences and scripts

## Discovery sequence (validation)

**Email 1 — research ask**  
See `research/customer-discovery-programme.md`.

**Email 2 — artefact ask**  
“When we speak, could we look at the structure of the last questionnaire (redacted is fine)? I’m mapping where time actually goes.”

**Email 3 — breakup**  
“Closing the loop — if customer packs aren’t a pain right now, that’s useful data too. Happy to reconnect in 6 months.”

## Pilot sequence (post pain confirmation)

**Email A**  
“Based on packs like {{type}} taking ~{{hours}} hours, we’re running three paid pilots in Norfolk this quarter: 4 weeks, up to 3 packs, £500 ex VAT, your approver signs everything. Interested?”

**Email B**  
“Sharing the pilot scope one-pager. No win-rate claims — we measure minutes and completeness.”

## Call openers

- “Walk me through the last pack — not the ideal process.”  
- “What did you almost send wrong?”  
- “Who would block uploading files to a vendor?”
