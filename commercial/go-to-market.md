# 18. Go-to-market (founder-led)

**As-of:** 9 November 2025  
No paid advertising until message and pilot conversion validated (target ≥30% pilot→paid among offered).

## First 50 named-account selection criteria

1. Norfolk registered / operating site  
2. Engineering, fabrication, machining, maintenance, marine/offshore services  
3. 5–75 employees (LinkedIn/site/Companies House signals)  
4. Visible multi-customer B2B supply (OEM, energy, construction main contractors)  
5. Mentions of ISO / SSIP / Constructionline / quality on website *(optional signal)*  
6. Reachable decision-maker (MD, Ops, Quality)  

Sources: NAAME, Hethel tenants, EEEZ matrix companies, local directories — **no implied partnership**. Network peer-intro orchestration (event + LinkedIn; not a new cold-50): [`naame-hethel-gy-network-peer-intro-pack.md`](naame-hethel-gy-network-peer-intro-pack.md).

## Buyer titles

MD, Managing Director, Operations Director, Quality Manager, Commercial Manager, Office Manager (small firms).

1. Last pack story (interview guide)  
2. Frequency  
3. Show empty wireframe / concierge method — not vapour feature tour  
4. Pilot offer if pain clear  
5. Book observation or pilot kickoff

1. Their pack types (3 min)  
2. Library → checklist → gap → approve → export (7 min)  
3. Security/approval principle (3 min)  
4. Pilot terms (2 min)  

Fixture-led runbook: [`concierge-discovery-demo-runbook.md`](concierge-discovery-demo-runbook.md) — redacted PQQ; checklist→MISSING→Approver; no SaaS. 

## Paid-pilot offer

4 weeks · max 3 packs · £250–£750 ex VAT · DPA required · see `research/pilot-design.md`.

|-----------|--------|

| SharePoint | Measure hours; pilot proves delta |
| AI risk | Abstain + human approve; export gates |
| Too busy | That’s the pain; pilot caps at 3 packs |
