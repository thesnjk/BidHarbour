## They ask “what is this / are you selling?”

Straight answer: this call is **customer discovery research** for Supplier Desk — a supplier-side tool idea for assembling qualification packs. I’m not selling software on the call. If the problem isn’t real for you, that’s useful data too. Still happy to talk for 25 minutes about your last pack.

---

## They say not interested / no time

Thanks for the straight answer — helpful. If a colleague at a similar Norfolk fab/marine firm might spare 25 minutes on how they handle customer questionnaires, a name/intro would be gold. Either way, appreciate it.

*(CRM: `outreach_status=declined` or leave Contacted + note; log in negative-findings if they say “not a problem for us”.)*

## They refer someone

Thanks — I’ll reach out to {{Referral}} and mention you suggested it. OK to use your name?

## They want a pilot / demo immediately

Glad there’s interest. Two tracks: (1) 25-min research call on your last pack first so we measure the real workflow; (2) paid pilot only after that, with solicitor-cleared terms — I won’t take confidential packs until those are ready. Shall we book the research call?
