## Goal

Recover missed discovery bookings and densify the same week toward ≥1 **held** qualified discovery — without treating booked-only as proof, and without a new cold scrape.

## Sequence

1. **Mark grid** — Set Booking slots Status to `No-show` or `Cancelled` (honest local truth). Do not leave as Open/Booked.  
2. **Same-day rebook (one attempt)** — Reply with the “yes / times” pattern from [`reply-booking-scripts.md`](reply-booking-scripts.md) (email or LinkedIn). One polite attempt; no chase spam.  
3. **On confirm** — New or reused Slot ID on the grid; CRM `stage=Discovery`, `outreach_status=booked`; send-log `reply_date` / `booked_slot` if applicable.  
4. **Densify (if still &lt;1 held this week)** — Prefer warm intros / referral queue, then Track B recycle from [`post-first50-referral-wave.md`](post-first50-referral-wave.md). Do **not** scrape a new cold 50. Anti-persona / reserve ≠ lead win.  
5. **Scorecard** — Update [`../research/qualified-discovery-week-tracker.md`](../research/qualified-discovery-week-tracker.md); Repeatable acquisition Actual **only** after a **held** interview (not booked-only). Soft decline → [`referral-ask-script.md`](referral-ask-script.md).

## Founder checklist

- [ ] Grid Status updated (`No-show` / `Cancelled`)  
- [ ] One same-day rebook attempt sent (or decline noted)  
- [ ] On confirm: grid + CRM + send-log aligned  
- [ ] If week still short: densify from warm/referral/Track B — not new cold scrape  
- [ ] Scorecard Actual only after held interview  
- [ ] Soft decline → one referral ask  

## Stop rules

- Never invent no-shows, rebooks, or held interviews.  
- Booked ≠ held — do not credit Repeatable acquisition on booking alone.  
- Never scrape a new cold 50-list for densification.  
- Never count anti-persona / reserve rows as lead ICP wins.  
- Never pitch SaaS hard-sell on rebook calls.  
- Soft decline → referral ask; do not harass.
