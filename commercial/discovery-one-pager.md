# Supplier Desk — one-pager (discovery / adviser)

**Product:** Supplier Desk (working company name: BidHarbour)  
**Tagline:** From customer request to submission-ready.  
**As-of:** 9 November 2025  
**Status:** Validation — not selling production SaaS yet.

## What it is

A practical supplier-side administrative assistant for small UK engineering, fabrication and marine suppliers who repeatedly assemble **different customers’ qualification questionnaires** (PQQ/SQ/assurance packs).

## Who it’s for (first)

Owner-managed firms in **Norfolk** (Norwich, Great Yarmouth, King’s Lynn corridors), roughly **5–75 people**, selling to multiple OEMs, tier-1s or asset operators.

## The job

1. Receive an incoming customer pack  
2. Map requirements to a checklist  
3. Match **approved** company evidence and answers  
4. Flag missing, expired or conflicting items  
5. Route to a **named human approver**  
6. Export a complete pack with an audit trail  

## What it is not

Not a supplier directory, buyer portal, certification scheme, generic file store, or AI that invents compliance answers. No guarantee of qualification or contract awards.

## Why existing tools often aren’t enough *(hypotheses to test)*

- Constructionline-style registers hold **one** profile — not every customer’s different template  
- Tender AI tools focus on **public** bids  
- SharePoint stores files but doesn’t enforce checklist → match → approve → export  

## Evidence so far

- Norfolk advanced manufacturing & engineering is a large local cluster (Business Board sector stats). *(Verified fact — see strategy docs.)*  
- Willingness to pay for pack assembly in adjacent tender tools is public (£49–£179/mo bands). *(Verified fact — competitor pricing 2025-11-09.)*  
- **Unverified:** pack frequency, hours, and WTP among Norfolk SMEs — that is what discovery interviews measure.

## Ask

25–40 minutes on the **last** customer questionnaire you completed — recent behaviour and documents, not hypotheticals. Optional paid 4-week pilot later if the pain is real ([`pilot-offer-sheet.md`](pilot-offer-sheet.md)).
