## Wrong fight (one line)

Public **tender discovery** and AI bid-win drafting are **out of scope by design** — Velstrian/KimonBids Lead there. Supplier Desk job = **inbound customer PQQ/SQ pack assembly** with evidence match, **MISSING** abstention, and human Approver export.

## Rival notes (short)

### Constructionline

- **Lead thesis:** multi-customer packs vs one membership profile.  
- **Do not claim:** “replaces Constructionline.”  
- **Do say:** complementary job — inbound OEM/customer questionnaires.

### SharePoint / M365 / Copilot

- **Lead thesis:** checklist + MISSING + Approver export vs folder scatter.  
- **Proof path only:** interviews + baseline + live minutes rollup (Actuals still 0).  
- **Do not** cite dry-run wall-clock as customer minutes saved.

### Tender-AI (Velstrian / KimonBids)

- **Lead thesis:** MISSING abstention + Approver vs invented tender prose.  
- **Wrong fight:** public tender find/draft.  
- Competitor mid-tiers (dated 2026-11-10 context in competitors.md) are **not** BidHarbour price proof.

### Complys

- **Lead thesis:** OEM inbound packs with Approver gate vs contractor RAMS/vault orientation.  
- **Copycat risk:** “customer pack importer” — monitor; don’t invent feature parity claims.

---

## Principles to repeat

1. AI may extract / match / draft. **Only authorised humans approve** statements and submissions.  
2. Unsupported facts → **MISSING** — never invent certifications, insurance, financials, or contractual commitments.  
3. Multi-customer: buyer-specific answers stay **scoped** (FO7) — no silent shared pollution.  
4. Differentiation valuable enough to buy? **Unproven** until paid pilots show time saved vs SharePoint.

---

## Stop rules

- No win-rate, qualification, or contract-award claims.  
- No invented scorecard Actuals or competitor “we’re cheaper” math.  
- No hard SaaS sell pre-GO; concierge pilot first.  
- No confidential packs without solicitor-cleared MSA/DPA.  
- Do not compete on estimating (RFQ tools) or holiday ops.
