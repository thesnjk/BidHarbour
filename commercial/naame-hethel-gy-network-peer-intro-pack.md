### Pre-event (5 min)

- [ ] Observation kit + checklist open  
- [ ] Signature / LinkedIn profile ready  
- [ ] CRM IDs listed (hope-to-meet — blank if none)  
- [ ] Consent / redaction rule ready  
- [ ] Calendar holds on [`../research/interview-grid-8wk.md`](../research/interview-grid-8wk.md)

### Same-day after event

1. Observation notes + session log if observation happened.  
2. For each useful peer chat: LinkedIn connect (script A) **or** warm email if they gave a published inbox.  
3. Ask for **1–2** peer intros if rapport exists (script B / spoken).  
4. CRM: new `Identified` · `queued` · `notes=referral_from={{event_or_id}}` — or update existing row.  
5. On book → Discovery; add grid slot.

## LinkedIn scripts (≤300 chars where short)

Replace `{{…}}`. Do not invent names.

### A — Post-event connection note

```text
Good to meet at {{event / venue}} — {{Founder}}, Supplier Desk discovery
(Norfolk fab/marine PQQ/SQ packs). Research only; not selling software today.
Open to a short follow-up if useful?
```

### B — Peer ask (after connection accepted / useful chat)

```text
Thanks again. Do you know 1–2 peers at similar Norfolk AME/marine firms who
also wrestle with multi-customer qualification packs? Happy for a warm intro
or a name + company — research framing only.
```

### C — Second-degree handoff (to the referred contact)

```text
{{Referrer}} suggested I reach out — {{Founder}}, Supplier Desk discovery.
Looking for 25 min on how inbound PQQ/SQ packs get assembled (redacted OK).
Research only; no win-rate claims. Open to a short call?
```

If email preferred: use warm-intro outbound pattern in [`referral-ask-script.md`](referral-ask-script.md) (consent for name-use; published inbox or LinkedIn only).

### Spoken peer ask at event (~20s)

```text
Before I let you go — anyone else here (or nearby) who also spends hours on
customer questionnaires? A warm intro would help the research.
```
