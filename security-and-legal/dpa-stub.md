## Subject matter

Processing of documents and related personal data uploaded to Supplier Desk / concierge pilot for the purpose of preparing customer qualification response packs.

Term of pilot/subscription + retention period, then deletion/return.

Account data; documents that may include names, contact details, insurance, HR/training records, etc., as supplied by Customer.

Instructions; confidentiality; security measures; subprocessors; international transfers; assistance with data subject rights; breach notice; deletion/return; audits.
