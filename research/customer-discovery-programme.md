# 9. Customer-discovery programme (8 weeks)

**As-of:** 9 November 2025  
Targets are **proposed research targets**, not statistical proof.

## Targets

| Activity | Target |
|----------|--------|
| Buyer interviews | 30 across 3 niches (~10 each) |
| Niches | (1) AME/fabrication (2) Marine/offshore services (3) Construction trades (control) |
| Workflow observations | ≥3 per niche (9 total stretch; **minimum 3 in lead niche**) |
| Paid pilots | 3 in strongest niche after week-4 gate |

## Recruitment methods

1. NAAME meeting attend / follow-up (no implied endorsement) — field kit: [`observation-kit-naame-hethel.md`](observation-kit-naame-hethel.md)  
2. Hethel Engineering Centre network introductions — same kit  
3. LinkedIn search: Norfolk MD / Quality Manager + fabrication/marine keywords  
4. EEEZ / ORE Catapult alumni / F4OR company lists (public)  
5. Warm intros from any founder contacts  
6. Made Smarter East adoption programme contacts where publicly approachable  

## Outreach script (email)

Subject: Quick question about customer questionnaires (Norfolk)

Hi {{Name}},

I’m researching how Norfolk engineering and marine suppliers handle customer qualification questionnaires and document packs — not selling software on this call.

Would you spare 25 minutes this or next week to walk through the **last** pack you completed? Happy to share a one-page summary of patterns (anonymised) afterwards.

Thanks,  
{{Founder}}

## Bias controls

- Ask for last occurrence, not typical  
- Request artefacts before pitching  
- Log disconfirming evidence in negative findings log  
- Do not count polite enthusiasm as WTP  
- Separate interviewer from note-coder when possible (Scenario C)

## Negative findings method

Every interview ends with: “What would make this **not** worth paying for?” Log in `research/negative-findings.md`.

## Decision thresholds

See `research/validation-scorecard.md` and week-4 / week-8 gates in `strategy/roadmap.md`.

## Stop / pivot criteria

- Week 4: &lt;4/10 in lead niche confirm recurring pain → test RFQ+compliance hybrid **or** STOP — run [`week4-gate-checklist.md`](week4-gate-checklist.md)  
- Week 8: &lt;3 paid pilots → STOP or major pivot — run [`week8-gate-checklist.md`](week8-gate-checklist.md)  
- Confidentiality blocks all real packs → STOP SaaS thesis or redesign delivery model
