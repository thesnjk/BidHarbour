# Customer interview guide

**Duration:** 25–40 minutes  
**Rule:** Recent behaviour and actual documents. No hypothetical enthusiasm as purchase proof.

## Consent (1 min)

“I’ll take notes / record for research. We can stop anytime. I won’t name your customers in anything shared externally without permission. OK?”

## Firm snapshot (3 min)

1. What does the business make or do?  
2. Rough employee count?  
3. Which software do you use for files and email?  
4. Do you hold ISO / SSIP / Constructionline / other schemes?

## Last pack deep-dive (15 min)

5. Tell me about the **most recent** customer questionnaire or document pack you completed. When?  
6. Who sent it (type of customer, not necessarily name)?  
7. What formats (PDF, portal, Excel)?  
8. Who worked on it, step by step?  
9. How long did it take, roughly, in hours?  
10. What took the longest?  
11. What went wrong or nearly went wrong (missing, expired, conflicting)?  
12. Can you show a redacted example or screenshare folder structure?

## Frequency and variety (5 min)

13. How many such packs in the last 12 months?  
14. How many different customers send different templates?  
15. Seasonal spikes?

## Current substitutes (5 min)

16. What do you use today (folders, SharePoint, consultant, Constructionline, other software)?  
17. What does that **not** do well for these packs?  
18. Have you tried tender/bid AI tools? Result?

## Money and authority (5 min)

19. Who would approve a £150/mo tool for this?  
20. Have you paid anyone (consultant/scheme) to help with packs in the last year? How much?  
21. If a pilot saved half the time on the next 3 packs for £500, would you try it? **Why / why not?**

## Close

22. Who else in Norfolk should I speak with?  
23. What would make this **not** worth paying for?

## Coding after call

- Pain same as ICP? Y/N  
- Packs/year estimate  
- Hours last pack  
- WTP signal: Yes paid interest / Soft / No  
- Negative finding  
- Evidence label for each coded field
