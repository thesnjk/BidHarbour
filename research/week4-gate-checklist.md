## 3. Decision matrix

| Pain rate (lead niche) | Workflow ≥3 businesses | Decision |
|------------------------|------------------------|----------|
| ≥6/10 | Yes | Continue; **prepare** paid pilots (NARROW niche OK). Do not claim scorecard GO for SaaS. |
| ≥6/10 | No | Keep discovering until ≥3 workflow firms; soft pilot mentions only. |
| ≥4/10 and &lt;6/10 | — | **NARROW** / keep discovering; **do not** launch paid pilots yet. |
| &lt;4/10 | — | **PIVOT** (RFQ+compliance hybrid only if explicitly reconsidering) or **STOP**. Do not silent-pivot to holiday ops. |

Threshold sources: scorecard ideal ≥6/10; grid Week-4 floor ≥4/10 or PIVOT/STOP; discovery programme &lt;4/10 → RFQ hybrid or STOP.
