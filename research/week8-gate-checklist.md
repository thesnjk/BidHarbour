## 3. Decision matrix

| Paid pilots | Continuations ≥2 | Time/value pass | Decision |
|-------------|------------------|-----------------|----------|
| 3 | Yes | Yes | Consider **GO MVP** (founder confirms before any SaaS scaffold). NARROW niche OK. |
| 3 | Yes | No | **NARROW** — prove value on next packs before GO MVP. |
| 3 | No | — | **NARROW** / keep operating; do not claim GO MVP until ≥2 written continuations. |
| 1–2 | — | — | **NARROW** or extend pilots; discovery programme: Week 8 &lt;3 paid pilots → **STOP** or major pivot. |
| 0 | — | — | **STOP** or major **PIVOT** (RFQ+compliance hybrid only if explicitly reconsidering). Do not silent-pivot to holiday ops. |

Also fail GO if confidentiality blocked all real packs → STOP SaaS thesis or redesign delivery (discovery programme).

Threshold sources: scorecard (3 pilots, ≥2 continuations, time/value, repeat usage); grid Week-8 outcome GO MVP / NARROW / PIVOT / STOP; discovery programme &lt;3 paid pilots → STOP or major pivot.
