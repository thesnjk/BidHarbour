## Conclusions

1. **Underserved job (hypothesis):** inbound, customer-specific qualification pack with approved evidence graph + human approval export at SME price.  
2. **Differentiation valuable enough to buy?** Unproven — must show time saved vs SharePoint in paid pilots.  
3. **Service vs SaaS:** Concierge + light software first; pure SaaS only after retention.  
4. **Most likely copycat:** Complys or a tender-AI vendor adding “customer pack importer.” Monitor quarterly.
