# 16. Pricing and business model

**As-of:** 9 November 2025  
All list prices **exclusive of VAT** unless noted. Hypotheses — not validated market prices.

## Experiments to run

| Offer | Band | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| 4-week paid pilot | £250–£750 | WTP + delivery cost learning |
| Setup / import / configuration | £300–£1,000 | Cover onboarding labour |
| Subscription | £99–£249 / month | Recurring |

## Model comparison

| Model | Pros | Cons | Fit |
|-------|------|------|-----|
| Per organisation | Simple | Ignores seats/volume | **Initial recommend** |
| Per seat | Aligns multi-user | SMEs hate seat counting | Later if needed |
| Per request | Aligns usage | Spiky revenue; billing friction | Overage only |
| Volume bands | Fairness | Complexity | Should after data |
| Managed + software | Matches concierge | Bespoke trap | **Pilot phase** |
| Hybrid | Flexibility | Hard to explain | OK if boundaries clear |

## Recommended initial offer

1. **Pilot:** £500 ex VAT (anchor mid)  
2. **Setup:** £500–£800 ex VAT (library import)  
3. **Core sub:** £149 / month ex VAT — 15 packs / 500 pages / 3 users / 1 approver  
4. **Plus:** £249 / month — 40 packs / 1,500 pages / 6 users / 2 approvers  

### Usage limits and overage

- Soft warn at 80%  
- Overage £15 / pack or page-credit top-up *(hypothesis)*  
- Hard stop optional for unpaid overage  

### Support boundaries

- Email support Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00 UK  
- No same-day custom Word templating in Core  
- Onboarding hours capped in setup fee  

### Annual payment

- 10× monthly (≈2 months free) *(hypothesis)*  
- No discount beyond annual without written rule  

### Discount rules

- Max 20% for multi-year or referral cohort — founder approval  
- Never discount below contribution-positive floor (see model)  

### Custom work

- Quote separately at £75–£120 / hour *(hypothesis)*  
- Does not include inventing compliance content  

## Link to value and cost

Value proxy: hours saved × internal loaded cost (e.g. £25–£45/h admin).  
If pilot saves 4 hours/pack × 3 packs = 12 hours → £300–£540 value vs £500 fee — test messaging carefully.  
Delivery cost must include founder hours (`finance/model-assumptions.md`).
