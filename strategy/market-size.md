## Definitions

- **Enterprise** = overall business (prefer this for SAM/SOM).  
- **Local unit / site** = workplace — do not treat as separate buyers.  
- **Geographic scope initial:** Norfolk only.  
- **Expansion:** East of England after Norfolk repeatability; UK only after that.

## Reproducible data-collection method (to complete sizing)

1. Nomis UK Business Counts → Norfolk enterprises by SIC 25–33, 71.1, 71.2, 33.1, 52.22 (review annually).  
2. Deduplicate enterprises (not local units).  
3. Sample 30 firms; measure pack frequency and multi-customer count.  
4. Apply observed prevalence % instead of 40%/50% assumptions.  
5. Recompute SAM/SOM; update confidence.

## Tool / data limitations
