## Why the leading niche wins

Offshore/marine + manufacturing/engineering combine: (1) verified local density, (2) multi-customer private qualification friction, (3) accessible networks (NAAME, Hethel, EEEZ), (4) willingness-to-pay analogies in adjacent compliance/tender spend.

## What would change the ranking

- Interviews show &lt;4 packs/year median → demote; consider RFQ+compliance hybrid or STOP.  
- Construction firms show stronger WTP and clearer budget → promote construction niche.  
- Confidentiality blocks cloud for marine → require on-prem/private deployment (likely kills solo SaaS unit economics).
