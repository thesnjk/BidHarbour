## Decision (validation period — through Week-4 / Week-8 gates)

| Item | Decision |
|------|----------|
| Repo / working company name | Keep **BidHarbour** |
| Customer-facing product name in all outreach | **Supplier Desk** |
| `bidharbour.com` | Do **not** buy, brand against, or invest in — Portuguese auction platform occupies it *(Verified fact)* |
| Paid ads / logo merch / incorporation under BidHarbour | **Defer** until IPO Exact+Similar screenshots + solicitor advice |
| Full rename | **Defer until after Week-4 gate**, unless IPO search shows a blocking UK mark |
| Reserve name / domain | Pursue **HarbourResponse** — register `harbourresponse.com` and/or `harbourresponse.co.uk` if still available *(desk: `.com` WHOIS “No match”, 2025-11-09)* |

## Why this split

1. **Auction confusion risk** on “BidHarbour” + taken `.com` would muddy discovery emails and Google results. Leading with **Supplier Desk** describes the job without claiming the contested brand.  
2. **Validation must not wait** on full TM clearance — interviews are the bottleneck (scorecard still 0/10).  
3. **HarbourResponse** keeps a memorable harbour metaphor if a later rename is forced, without blocking Week-1 send.  
4. Renaming the GitHub repo mid-validation creates noise; keep BidHarbour as the internal working name until evidence justifies the cost.

## What this does *not* mean

- UK IPO / Companies House / EUIPO are **not** cleared.  
- HarbourResponse is **not** guaranteed available at purchase time — confirm in Nominet / registrar UI before paying.  
- No endorsement that BidHarbour is safe to trademark.
