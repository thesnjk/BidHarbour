## Pre-mortem

The business failed after 12 months because packs were too infrequent to justify a subscription, customers kept using SharePoint plus Constructionline, one AI mismatch damaged trust, and bespoke export formatting consumed all margin while the founder stopped prospecting to keep pilots alive.
