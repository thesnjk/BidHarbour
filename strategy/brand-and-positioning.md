- Product tagline: **From customer request to submission-ready.**  
- Works with product name **Supplier Desk**.

## Product naming hierarchy (simple — no sub-brand sprawl)

1. **Company / brand:** BidHarbour *(working)* or chosen rename  
2. **Product:** Supplier Desk *(use this in emails)*  
3. **Modules (later only if needed):** e.g. Library, Requests, Approvals — not separate brands

## Positioning statement

For Norfolk and East of England engineering, fabrication and marine suppliers who repeatedly assemble different customers’ qualification packs, **Supplier Desk** is the supplier-side administrative assistant that maps requirements to approved evidence, flags gaps, and exports a reviewer-approved pack — unlike registers, buyer portals or tender-writing tools that either store one profile or draft answers without evidence discipline.

## Homepage

- **Headline:** From customer request to submission-ready.  
- **Subheading:** Map requirements, match your approved evidence, flag gaps, export — without inventing compliance.

## Thirty-second sales pitch

“Your customers each send a different questionnaire. You keep re-finding the same certificates and policies. Supplier Desk extracts what they’re asking for, matches your approved library, flags what’s missing or expired, and routes a draft to a named approver before you export the pack.”

## Tone of voice

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Practical, calm, audit-friendly | “AI wins bids” |
| Precise about human approval | “Guaranteed qualification” |
| Honest about missing evidence | “Fully automated compliance” |
| Norfolk/UK SME plain English | Hype, purple-glow tech marketing |

## Messaging that avoids overstatement

- AI **assists** extraction, matching and drafting.  
- Humans **approve** statements and submissions.  
- We help prepare **complete packs**; we do **not** certify, accredit or guarantee awards.  
- Where evidence is missing, we **mark missing** — we do not invent.
